In Metropolitan City, Mark Grayson navigates the dual pressures of high school and his newfound abilities. His father, Omni-Man, is the world's most powerful hero, a legacy that now falls to Mark as he learns to control his own strength. The routine of classes and social anxieties is constantly interrupted by threats from the city's supervillains. Each confrontation tests his limits, forcing him to balance the chaos of adolescence with the immense responsibility of his powers. The skyline is a frequent backdrop for his clumsy, high-velocity learning curve.

profitonia★ 5/5Review
An easy contender for my favorite comic, Kirkman really did something special with this story. From the start, it is so captivating, and it really does not slow down. Mark is one of the best protagonists in any sort of story I've had the pleasure of consuming. His progression from a frail high school student to the emperor of a warrior race and proud father is such a pleasure to witness. The supporting cast is just as great; Eve, Nolan, and Robot are standouts in this regard. Look at any of these three characters at the start of their story and then at their end, and you'll see completely different people. And while some of the humor hasn't stood the test of time, it really doesn't take away from the story. I think what makes this story so special is that it ends, something that can't be said about many of the characters we've come to know and love through this medium. I haven't even touched the tip of the iceberg regarding just how good this story is, nor have I mentioned how damn good Ottley and Walker are. I've recommended this story a thousand times over to comic fans and non-fans alike and will continue to do so.. Seriously, give it a shot.
TyrantTyler📖 read 170 ch. ★ 5/5Short take
Invincible is the ultimate superhero comic. It is a loveletter to the genre and you can feel the passion in every dialogue, panel, and issue. It stayed true to its roots until the very end and is a joy to read every night at 1AM, spamming the right side of my screen and immersing myself in the world of Invincible and its unique and amazing characters. From start to finish, it was a masterpiece and I don’t think any single superhero verse comes close to the level of perfection and storytelling Invincible has done with a single run of 144 chapters. Its ending was extremely satisfying and wrapped up everyone’s character arc nicely with no (major) loose ends. So thank you Invincible, you will go down in history as one of the greatest superhero comics ever.
jjujubuju📖 read 7 ch. ★ 5/5Short take
this series was just phenomenal. the way we see every character develop into their own, good or bad, is so satisfying and beautiful in its special way. seeing mark grayson struggle, love, fear, fight, and just exist as his own person felt so raw and personal with each step he took. we see themes of sacrifice and justice constantly evolving throughout the entire series, watching each character develop their own moral compass. the viltrumites working to become more than who they were was something satisfying as a reader. even in the aftermath of everything that’s happened, this series has so much potential and yet it’s amazing as it is. i’m so proud of the person mark became and the good he’s done, this entire storyline was worth it from the first page til the last panel. i will be rereading this so often, this has become one of my ultimate favorites.
